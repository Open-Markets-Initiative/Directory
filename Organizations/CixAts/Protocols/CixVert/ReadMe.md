## CIX ASPEN VERT


CIX ASPEN VERT, a lit continuous limit order book for Canadian listed equities (TSX, TSXV, CBOE, CSE) on the CIX alternative trading system operated by CIX Trading Inc. Near-identical to CIX ASPEN with taker/maker pricing and fractional and odd-lot share trading, matching via CIX's per-security optimization cycle.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
