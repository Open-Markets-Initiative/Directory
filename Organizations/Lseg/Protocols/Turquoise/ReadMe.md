## Turquoise


Turquoise is the Lseg pan European multilateral trading facility (Mtf), operating the Turquoise Lit, Turquoise Plato and Turquoise Plato Lit Auctions order books under Turquoise Global Holdings Limited (Tghl) in the United Kingdom and Turquoise Global Holdings Europe (Tghe) in the European Union.
