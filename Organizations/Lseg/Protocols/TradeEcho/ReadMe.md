## TRADEcho


TRADEcho is the Lseg trade reporting and publication service, operating an Approved Publication Arrangement (Apa) for MiFID II post trade transparency reports and publishing Systematic Internaliser (Si) quotes on behalf of investment firms.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Level2](Level2.md) | MarketData | Lseg Trade Echo Depth Of Book Data |
| [Level2Incremental](Level2Incremental.md) | MarketData | Level 2 Incremental |
| [Level2Recovery](Level2Recovery.md) | MarketData | Level 2 Recovery |
| [Level2Replay](Level2Replay.md) | MarketData | Level 2 Replay |
| [Mifid](Mifid.md) | MarketData | MiFID II Post Trade |
| [MifidRecovery](MifidRecovery.md) | MarketData | MiFID II Post Trade Recovery |
| [MifidReplay](MifidReplay.md) | MarketData | MiFID II Post Trade Replay |
