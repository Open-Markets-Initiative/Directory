## HkexDerivatives Combined: Orion Market Data Derivatives Combined

Hkex Orion Market Data Derivatives Combined is the superset variant, carrying every OMD-D message across all derivatives products. Intended for captures that mix products or for development/QA where a single dissector is preferred over per-product dissectors.

### Transport

Udp multicast delivery on dual A and B channels with per-channel sequence numbers.
