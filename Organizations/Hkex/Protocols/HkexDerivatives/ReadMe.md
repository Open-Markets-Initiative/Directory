## Hkex Derivatives Market


The Hkex Derivatives Market is operated by Hong Kong Futures Exchange Limited (HKFE), a wholly owned subsidiary of Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing (Hkex). It covers stock and equity index futures and options, interest-rate and fixed-income futures, and commodity, currency, and gold futures and options. Market data is disseminated via the Orion Market Data - Derivatives (OMD-D) platform.
