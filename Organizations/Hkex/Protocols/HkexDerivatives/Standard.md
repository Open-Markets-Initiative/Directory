## HkexDerivatives Standard: Orion Market Data Derivatives Standard

Hkex Orion Market Data Derivatives Standard (DS) is the aggregated depth-of-book derivatives market data product, delivering aggregate order book updates, trades, calculated opening prices, implied volatility, open interest, series and trade statistics, and market alerts across the Hkex derivatives market. DS omits order-level Add/Modify/Delete events (available in FullTick).

### Transport

Udp multicast delivery on dual A and B channels with per-channel sequence numbers.
