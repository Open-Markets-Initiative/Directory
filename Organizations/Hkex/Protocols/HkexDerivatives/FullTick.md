## HkexDerivatives Full Tick: Orion Market Data Derivatives FullTick

Hkex Orion Market Data Derivatives FullTick (DF) is the full order-level derivatives product, publishing per-order Add/Modify/Delete events, aggregate implied orders, trades, calculated opening prices, implied volatility, open interest, and market alerts.

### Transport

Udp multicast delivery on dual A and B channels with per-channel sequence numbers.
