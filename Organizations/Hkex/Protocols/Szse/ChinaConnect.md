## Szse China Connect: Orion Market Data China Connect

UDP multicast real-time China Connect (OMD-CC) market data for Shenzhen Stock Exchange (SZSE)-listed A-Share securities eligible for Northbound trading, redistributed by Hkex to CCEPs and ASPs. Carries Sequence Reset, DR Signal, Market Definition, Security Definition, Security Status, Top Of Book, and Statistics messages. MarketCode within messages is ASZR.

### Transport

Udp multicast delivery on dual A/B channels dedicated to the Shenzhen Stock Exchange (SZSE) market.
