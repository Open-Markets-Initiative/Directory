## Szse China Connect Refresh: Orion Market Data China Connect Refresh

UDP multicast refresh (snapshot) service for the China Connect (OMD-CC) Shenzhen Stock Exchange (SZSE) feed, publishing periodic state snapshots so a late-joining client can rebuild current security definition, status, and top-of-book state.

### Transport

Udp multicast refresh channel for the Shenzhen Stock Exchange (SZSE) market.
