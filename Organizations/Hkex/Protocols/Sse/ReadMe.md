## Shanghai Stock Exchange


Shanghai Stock Exchange (SSE). Market data for SSE-listed A-Share securities eligible for Northbound trading under Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect is redistributed by Hkex via the OMD-CC (Orion Market Data - China Connect) datafeed to China Connect Exchange Participants (CCEPs) and ASPs.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ChinaConnect](ChinaConnect.md) | MarketData | Orion Market Data China Connect |
| [ChinaConnectRefresh](ChinaConnectRefresh.md) | MarketData | Orion Market Data China Connect Refresh |
| [ChinaConnectRetrans](ChinaConnectRetrans.md) | MarketData | Orion Market Data China Connect Retransmission |
