## ChinaConnect Order Entry: Hkex China Connect Central Gateway (CCCG) FIX Trading Interface

Financial Information eXchange (Fix 5.0 SP2) encoding of the Hkex China Connect Central Gateway (CCCG) trading interface, the market access platform used by Exchange Participants for northbound China Stock Connect trading of designated stocks listed on the Shanghai and Shenzhen stock exchanges.

### Transport

Tcp via the CCCG Fix session for reliable ordered delivery of northbound order entry, cancellation, and execution report messages.
