## HkexSecurities Order Entry: Hkex Orion Central Gateway - Securities Market (OCG-C) FIX Trading Interface

Financial Information eXchange (Fix 5.0 SP2) encoding of the Hkex Orion Central Gateway Securities Market (OCG-C) trading interface, the market access platform used by Exchange Participants to submit, modify and cancel orders and quotes and receive execution reports for the HKEX securities trading system (OTP-C).

### Transport

Tcp via the OCG-C Fix session for reliable ordered delivery of order entry, modification, cancellation, and execution report messages.
