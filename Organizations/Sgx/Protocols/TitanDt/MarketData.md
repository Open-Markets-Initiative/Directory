## TitanDt Market Data: Sgx Titan GeniumFIX Market Data

Financial Information eXchange (Fixt 1.1 session with Fix 5.0 SP2 application messages) encoding of the Sgx Titan GeniumFIX market data interface, streaming pre-trade and post-trade market data, index and equilibrium prices, news, circuit breaker details, trading session and security status for the Sgx derivatives markets.

### Transport

Tcp via the Sgx GeniumFIX session for reliable ordered delivery of market data messages, with application sequencing for recovery.
