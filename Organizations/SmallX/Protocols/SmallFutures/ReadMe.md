## Small Exchange


The Small Exchange futures market, a designated contract market listing small notional futures and options on futures across equity index, interest rate, metals, energy and foreign exchange. Contracts are deliberately sized and standardized so that a single product family covers an asset class, which is what makes the exchange reachable by traders for whom conventional futures are too large.
