## OdxEquities Order Entry: Odx Pts FIX Trading Specification for Equities

Financial Information eXchange (Fix 4.2) encoding of the Osaka Digital Exchange Pts equities trading interface, used by participants to submit, modify and cancel equity orders with Japanese cash/margin transaction fields and receive execution reports over the Odx Fix session.

### Transport

Tcp via the Odx Pts Fix session for reliable ordered delivery of order entry, cancellation, and execution report messages.
