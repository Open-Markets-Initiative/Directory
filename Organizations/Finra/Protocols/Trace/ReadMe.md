## Finra TRACE


Finra Trade Reporting And Compliance Engine, the facility through which Finra member firms report over the counter transactions in eligible fixed income securities under Finra Rule 6700 and from which the Trace transparency feeds are disseminated.

| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
