## AsxSecurities Trade.Order Entry: Asx Trade Equities Fix Order Entry

Financial Information eXchange (Fixt 1.1 session with Fix 5.0 application messages) order entry protocol for submitting, modifying and cancelling orders, requesting quotes and reporting trades on the Asx Trade cash equities platform.

### Transport

Tcp via the Asx Trade Fix session for reliable ordered delivery of order entry, modification, cancellation, quote request, trade capture and execution report messages.
