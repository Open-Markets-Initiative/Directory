## AsxDerivatives Ntp.Drop Copy: Asx 24 Derivatives Fix Drop Copy

Financial Information eXchange (Fixt 1.1 session with Fix 5.0 application messages) drop copy protocol delivering execution report and trade capture report copies for the Asx 24 derivatives market via the Asx New Trading Platform.

### Transport

Tcp via the Asx 24 New Trading Platform Fix session for reliable ordered delivery of execution report and trade capture report drop messages.
