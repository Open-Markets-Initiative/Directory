## AsxDerivatives Trade24: Asx Trade24 Legacy Fix Order Entry and Market Data

Financial Information eXchange (Fix 4.0) order entry and market data protocol for the Asx 24 derivatives market on the legacy Trade24 platform, superseded by the Asx New Trading Platform.

### Transport

Tcp via the Asx Fix session for reliable ordered delivery of order entry, execution report and user broadcast messages.
